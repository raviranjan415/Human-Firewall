Vision for Securing Humans from Modern Threats

"Over 90% of all successful cyber attacks do not hack computer code—they hack human decisions. In an era dominated by AI voice cloning, deepfake video executive impersonations, and sophisticated financial smishing, technical controls like firewalls and antivirus alone cannot protect us. The ultimate security perimeter is the human mind. My vision for Human Firewall is to equip every student, corporate employee, and citizen with verified defensive instincts, practical evidence inspection skills, and legal protection knowledge so that when high-pressure manipulation strikes, they pause, verify, and neutralize the threat."
— Ravi Ranjan, Creator & Developer
🎯 Engineering Philosophy

Education must be active, not passive. Instead of boring 45-minute slide decks, we use synchronized 30-second audio-visual simulations, hands-on evidence dissection, and instant human coaching hints.

⚖️ Legal Empowerment

Integrating India's IT Act 2000 (Section 66D), RBI Zero Liability 3-day reporting mandates, DPDP Act 2023, and the 1930 National Cybercrime Helpline into every practical lesson.

Why Human Firewall Works

A structured, science-backed methodology for building cognitive cyber immunity

🎬

1. 30-Sec Synced Video Briefings

Fast, synchronized visual simulations with bilingual narration in English and Hindi. Explains the attack vector in under 30 seconds without time-wasting fluff.

💬

2. Realistic Incident Chat Stories

Real human conversations showing the emotional pressure deployed by scammers (urgency, panic, fear of penalties) and the exact out-of-band verification steps that defeat them.

🧪

3. Hands-On Artifact Labs

Direct inspection of real evidence: spoofed email headers, fake SMS links, suspicious invoice PDFs, and terminal logs inside interactive browser sandboxes.

⚖️

4. Legal & Helpline Protections

Citizens and employees learn their statutory protections: RBI 3-day zero liability reporting, Section 66D penalties, and dialling 1930 to freeze stolen funds during the golden hour.
