Accessibility statement

CyberQuest is meant to be usable by a wide audience, from teenagers to older adults and people who are not technical. This page explains what we do to support that, and where we still have work to do.

We have designed the platform with accessibility in mind, but we do not yet claim full conformance with any formal standard. The “Known limitations” section below is deliberately honest about what is not finished.

What we support

  • Keyboard navigation with a visible focus ring. You can move through links, buttons and menus using only the keyboard, and the focused item is clearly outlined.
  • Skip-to-content link. Press it to jump straight past the navigation to the main content.
  • Semantic landmarks. Pages use proper headings, navigation, main and footer regions, so screen readers can move around the structure.
  • Text alternatives. Meaningful images and icons carry descriptive alt text or are marked as decorative, and icon-only controls have an accessible label.
  • Colour contrast. The default theme is built to keep text readable against the dark background, and meaning is never carried by colour alone.

Display and motion options

Three comfort settings are available, and they apply to every page once switched on. You can change them any time on the Settings page:

  • High-contrast mode — stronger text and border contrast for easier reading.
  • Large and extra-large text — increase the base text size of the whole site.
  • Reduce motion — turns off animations and transitions for people who find movement distracting or uncomfortable.

Known limitations

We would rather tell you plainly than over-claim:

  • Some narrative illustrations are still placeholders while the final artwork is produced.
  • Not all content is translated yet — Hindi coverage is growing, and some items may only appear in English for now.
  • Some interactive lab experiences are newer and have had less accessibility testing than the core pages.
  • We have not yet carried out a full audit against a formal standard.

Tell us about a barrier

If something is hard to use, please let us know through {{ t('footer.help') }}. Describe the page and what happened, and we will look into it.
