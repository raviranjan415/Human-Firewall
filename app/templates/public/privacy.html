Privacy policy

This page explains, in plain language, what CyberQuest collects, why it collects it, and the choices you have. It describes how this training platform is built to work.

Template policy. This text is a starting point written for a training platform. Review it with your own legal and data-protection advisers before you use it in production.

What we collect

  • Account details — a username, an e-mail address, an optional display name and your chosen language. We do not ask for your real name, address, phone number or payment details.
  • Learning progress — which lessons, labs, comics and games you have started or finished, your scores, and which prerequisites you have cleared.
  • XP, levels and streaks — the points and badges that make up your profile and leaderboard position.
  • Analytics events — anonymous-style usage signals such as which activities are opened and completed, used to improve the training.
  • Audit and security logs — sign-in events and similar records that help us keep the service safe and detect abuse.

Why we collect it

  • To run the training and remember where you are.
  • To show your progress, XP, streaks and achievements.
  • To issue certificates and let them be publicly verified.
  • To keep the service secure and to detect misuse.

What we never collect

CyberQuest teaches defensive awareness on simulated scenarios. Nothing on this site asks for real credentials. We never collect your real passwords, one-time passcodes (OTPs), PINs, card numbers or banking details, and we never ask you to enter third-party secrets. All scam examples, e-mails, messages and fake websites in the training are synthetic and created for teaching.

How passwords are stored

Account passwords are stored only as salted, one-way hashes. We cannot read your password back, and neither can anyone who might see the database. Multi-factor authentication adds a second check even if a password is ever guessed.

Cookies and sessions

  • A session cookie keeps you signed in while you use the platform.
  • A small preference is stored for your chosen language so the site stays in the language you picked.

These are needed for the site to work. We do not use them to build advertising profiles.

How long we keep it

Account and progress data is kept while your account is active, so your learning history and certificates stay intact. Security and audit logs are kept for a limited period needed to investigate problems, then removed. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your personal data unless we are required to keep a record for legal or security reasons.

Your choices

  • Download your data. You can export your own learning data at any time from your data export (sign in first).
  • Request deletion. Ask us to delete your account and personal data — see the contact route below.
  • Change your details. Update your profile and language from your account settings.

Contact

For any privacy question, a data export, or a deletion request, reach us through {{ t('footer.help') }}.

  • ✅ Passwords as salted one-way hashes

  • 📥 Export your data any time
