Featured skills

What you will learn

🎣

Phishing & spear phishing

Learn to slow down and check the sender, the link and the tone of a message before you click. We practise look-alike domains, urgent “verify your account” e-mails and messages that sound like they come from someone you know.

📞

Scam calls & messages

Spot the pressure tactics behind OTP requests, fake job offers and “your account will be blocked” frauds. You will see how the same scam changes its costume across SMS, WhatsApp-style chats and phone calls.

🎭

Deepfakes & impersonation

Understand why a familiar voice or face on a video call is no longer proof by itself, and learn simple verification habits — callback on a number you already trust, or ask a question only the real person could answer.

🔑

Passwords & account hygiene

Build the basics that break most attacks: long unique passwords, a password manager and multi-factor authentication. We explain why reusing one password puts every account at risk at the same time.

🌐

Safe browsing & privacy

Notice the small signals that separate a real website from a fake one, understand what permissions apps ask for, and make calmer, more informed choices about the information you share online.

🚨

Incident response

Everyone makes a mistake. Learn what to do in the first few minutes after clicking a bad link or sharing a code — change the password, turn on MFA, tell the right person — instead of hiding it and hoping.

Honest note: awareness training reduces risk, but no course can promise that you will never be targeted or never make a mistake. Our goal is to make good reactions faster and more automatic.
